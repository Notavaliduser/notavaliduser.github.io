### Overall Impression

The system is well-built. The content is genuinely good. But the architecture is a resume in HTML rather than a curated experience. The page front-loads its best moment and ends flat. The biggest opportunity is structural.

### What's Working

1. The philosophy blockquote — convergence of visual treatment and content quality creates the emotional peak.
2. Metric pills on case study cards front-load credibility at the scan layer.
3. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next is a genuine value statement, not a design convenience.
