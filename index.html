How I work

Constraints are design inputs. Legal, infrastructure, timelines shape the product. Some of my best decisions came from working within a constraint everyone else was trying to remove.
Ship before the foundation is perfect. If the window matters more than the polish, I ship. Deliberate debt, scoped and paid down. I'd rather fix real problems from real users than hypothetical ones in a planning doc.
Say what didn't work before anyone asks. Every case study here has a section on what went wrong, because the interesting part of a product decision is usually what you'd change. If you can't name your mistakes specifically, you probably haven't examined them.
Reverse course when the data says to. I've killed committed launches and pivoted strategies mid-flight when the numbers told a different story than the plan. The hard part is telling stakeholders their assumptions were wrong.
Leave the system better than the project. A survey methodology I introduced for one squad was adopted across four brands. A tooling fix for one feature became infrastructure other teams built on. A junior PM I mentored shipped the programme and was promoted. I think about what persists after the project ends.
